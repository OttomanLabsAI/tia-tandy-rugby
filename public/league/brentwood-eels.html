Because rugby league allows players to transition into senior women’s rugby at 17 — compared to 18 in union — Tia made a rapid jump to Brentwood Eels in the 2nd Division. Thriving under great coaching and a supportive environment, she put up exceptional running and tackling metrics, including a highlight-reel try scored from inside her own 22-metre line.

After a brief initial spell with London Broncos, Tia returned to the Eels for a second stint. Battling through tough, high-workrate matches where she frequently made back-to-back defensive stops, the experience tested her fitness, tackle timing and resilience before she shifted focus entirely to her senior union goals.
