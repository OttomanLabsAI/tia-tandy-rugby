Scouted out of the open-age game, Tia earned six caps for a Broncos squad that topped the Championship table and went within one game of Super League promotion — all while training full-time in Oxford, with away days as far as Sheffield, Liverpool, Leeds and Manchester.

The spell was brief by her own choice: league was keeping her match-sharp for union, and the logistics didn’t add up. Why it ended is best told in her words below.

Season by Season

Why the league chapter closed — in Tia’s own words.

2025–26London Broncos6 caps

The problem with playing for Broncos was travelling. I played in Sheffield, Liverpool, Leeds and Manchester — and even the home ground was over two hours away. I was in Oxford during the week, so the only session I could make was Friday evening: after training twice with Oxford, travelling back to London for 2pm, then waiting until 7pm for training. It just didn’t work for me, so I decided to go back and have a few more games with Eels.

I was only using league as a way to keep my match fitness and tackle timing up — so despite it being an honour to represent a team with the history and level of Broncos, I had to focus on my own plan.
