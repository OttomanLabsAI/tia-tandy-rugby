Tia Tandy has been running at defenders since she was eight years old. She picked the game up at Dartford Valley RFC — first on the grass at Central Park, later on the 3G at Cotton Lane — and stayed for a decade, playing every age grade through to Under-18s and collecting Kent League titles and cup wins along the way.

County rugby followed. Tia has pulled on the Kent shirt at U14, U16 and U18 level and now captains the side, while two years inside the Activate Oxford Rugby Academy’s elite programme sharpened her game for the senior stage.

The step up came fast. A switch to rugby league with Sidcup Sharks and Brentwood Eels got her scouted by London Broncos, where she broke into a squad that finished top of the Championship. In union — her main code — she has signed for Thurrock T-Birds, going into the season as the youngest front-row forward in the Women’s Championship. The ambition from here is simple: keep climbing, all the way to an England shirt.
