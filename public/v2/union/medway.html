Alongside her club rugby, Tia represented Medway RFC — valuable experience against strong opposition, and further development of her versatility across the forward pack.

Sixteen caps and nine tries over three seasons, until the sheer weight of her playing schedule forced a choice — the story is in the record below.

Season by Season

The record — in Tia’s own words.

U15Medway RFC7 caps · 3 tries

Played seven matches, scoring three tries while featuring at both hooker and Number 8.

U16Medway RFC7 caps · 4 tries

During my first U16 season, I played seven matches and scored four tries, continuing to perform primarily at hooker.

U16Medway RFC · second season2 caps · 2 tries

In my second U16 season, I played two matches and scored two tries. However, due to my heavy playing schedule across multiple teams — including occasions where I played twice in one day — the RFU advised that my playing load was becoming unsustainable. As a result, I stepped away from Medway to better manage my workload and long-term development.
