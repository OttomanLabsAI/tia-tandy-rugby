The home club. Tia picked the game up at Dartford Valley RFC at eight years old — first on the grass at Central Park, later on the 3G at Cotton Lane — and stayed for a decade, playing every age grade through to Under-18s.

Along the way: Kent League titles, cup wins, and the grounding that everything since has been built on. One club, one badge, ten years.
