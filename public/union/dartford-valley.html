The home club — in all its forms. Tia picked the game up at Dartford Valley RFC at eight years old and stayed through every change of shirt: the merger with Old Colfeians that created the Crimson Roses, then the addition of Beckenham that made Beccs Valley.

Forty-five caps and thirty-one tries later, she still turns out at weekends for Beccs mixed rugby — the season-by-season record is below.

Season by Season

The full record through every change of shirt — in Tia’s own words.

U12Dartford Valley RFC6 caps · 2 tries

My first season of competitive rugby saw me play six matches in the mixed U12s side. Playing as a forward, I scored two tries and quickly developed the fearless, physical playing style that has remained a key part of my game.

U14Dartford Valley / Crimson Roses8 caps · 5 tries

At U14 level, I played eight matches for Dartford Valley before the squad merged with Old Colfeians to form the Crimson Roses. The new team enjoyed a successful season, winning around 70% of its fixtures. Playing primarily at hooker, I scored five tries while continuing to develop my technical and physical game.

U15Crimson Roses9 caps · 4 tries

I remained with the Crimson Roses for the following season, making nine appearances and scoring four tries. During this campaign I continued to develop as a front-row forward and became an important member of the squad.

U16Crimson Roses9 caps · 4 tries · Kent Cup runners-up

In my first year of U16 rugby, the Crimson Roses reached the Kent Cup Final, finishing as runners-up. I played nine matches and scored four tries, further establishing myself as a reliable and physical hooker.

U16Beccs Valley5 tries · Kent Cup winners

In my second U16 season, Dartford Valley, Old Colfeians and Beckenham combined to form Beccs Valley. The new team enjoyed an outstanding campaign, winning the Kent Cup. I contributed five tries throughout the season.

U18Beccs Valley5 tries · unbeaten league & cup double

During my first season at U18 level, Beccs Valley completed an unbeaten campaign, winning both the Kent League and Kent Cup. I played a key role throughout the season, scoring five tries while continuing to develop as a versatile and powerful forward.

U18Beccs Valley · final season5 caps · 6 tries · first career hat-trick

In my final U18 season, injuries restricted me to just five appearances. Despite the limited game time, I scored six tries, including my first career hat-trick in the Kent Cup semi-final to help the team reach the final. We finished runners-up in the Kent Cup and second in the league.
