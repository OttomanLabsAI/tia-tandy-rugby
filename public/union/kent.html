Five years in the county pathway, from a 120-player U14 intake to a cut-down, genuinely competitive U18 squad of 32 — with the captain’s armband at U16, and again on her return at U18.

Season by Season

The record — in Tia’s own words.

U14Kent Rugby & PDG3 caps · 1 try

I joined the Kent pathway during my second year of U14 rugby. With over 120 players registered and only a limited fixture programme, opportunities were restricted. Matches were typically split into two halves, with the entire squad rotated at half-time, limiting playing time for everyone involved.

U16Kent Captain4 caps · 1 try

Competition for places remained extremely high, with around 120 players selected across four Kent regions. During my second year, balancing Kent and Oxford commitments became increasingly demanding, often involving an Oxford training session, travelling home, and then completing a further two-hour journey to Kent training.

Despite these challenges, I was appointed Kent captain at the end of the season. However, the travel and workload became unsustainable, and I decided not to represent Kent during my first year of U18 rugby.

U18Kent Captain4 caps · 2 tries

I returned during my second year of U18 rugby under a new coaching team and management structure. Following a fresh trial process, squad numbers had been reduced from around 120 players to just 32, creating a far more competitive and balanced environment.

After successfully earning my place back in the squad, I was reappointed captain. With regular full-match opportunities, I thoroughly enjoyed the season and felt I was able to contribute much more effectively both as a player and a leader.
