The Story

Built in Dartford

Tia Tandy has been running at defenders since she was eight years old. She picked the game up at Dartford Valley RFC — starting in mixed rugby, lining up against the boys in the U12s — and she has never really left: even now, weekends often end back at the Valley playing Beccs mixed rugby. Along the way came three seasons at Medway, Kent Sevens and Kent Cup wins, and an unbeaten league-and-cup double at U18.

County rugby followed — the Kent shirt at U14, U16 and U18, and now the U18s captaincy. By twelve she was training five and six times a week, working with her dad on sprints, swimming and ball skills; at sixteen she moved to train full-time at the Activate Oxford Rugby Academy while finishing her studies.

The step up came fast. A switch to rugby league with Sidcup Sharks and Brentwood Eels got her scouted by London Broncos, where she broke into a squad that finished top of the Championship. Now comes the big one: a 2026 senior debut for Thurrock T-Birds in the Women’s Championship, with the club chasing promotion to the Premiership — and Tia’s long-term sights set on an England shirt.

Grassroots Forever

Dartford Valley RFC since age eight — and still her club. Blue hair, one sock up, one sock down, and weekends that end where it all started.

The Grind

Full-time education. Five to six sessions a week. Weekend fixtures. Every mile by public transport — unpaid.
